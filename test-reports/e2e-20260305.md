## Key Technical Details

1. **USDC contract**: `0x1c7D4B196Cb0C7B01d743Fbc6116a902379C7238` (Circle, 6 decimals, Sepolia)

2. **Fully gasless Alice**: In E2E4337, Alice never holds ETH. Gas is paid from EntryPoint deposit, and USDC is transferred directly back to Sponsor via executeBatch.

3. **EIP-191 personal_sign**: UserOp hash wrapped with `\x19Ethereum Signed Message:\n32`.

4. **vm.setNonce workaround**: Both scripts use `vm.setNonce` after delegation to sync forge's simulation nonce with on-chain EIP-7702 auth nonce increment.

5. **Gas estimation**: `--gas-estimate-multiplier 500` needed for type 4 txs.
