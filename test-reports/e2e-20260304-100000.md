## Key Technical Details

1. **USDC contract**: `0x1c7D4B196Cb0C7B01d743Fbc6116a902379C7238` (Circle, 6 decimals, Sepolia)

2. **Fully gasless Alice**: In E2E4337, Alice never holds ETH. Gas is paid from EntryPoint deposit (Sponsor), and UserOp prefund is deducted from the deposit. Alice only interacts with USDC.

3. **EIP-191 personal_sign**: UserOp hash wrapped with `\x19Ethereum Signed Message:\n32` before ecrecover.

4. **Prefund gating**: Only pays EntryPoint prefund when signature is valid (`validationData == SIG_VALIDATION_SUCCESS`).

5. **vm.setNonce workaround**: In E2EDirect, EIP-7702 auth increments Alice's nonce but forge simulation doesn't model this. `vm.setNonce(alice, 1)` syncs.

6. **Gas estimation**: `--gas-estimate-multiplier 500` needed for type 4 txs.

7. **USDC return**: E2E4337 step 8 returns USDC from Deployer to Sponsor, keeping Sponsor balance intact across runs.
